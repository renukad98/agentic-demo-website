Small cookies.
Clear choices.

Necessary storage keeps your account working. Everything else is your choice, and you can change your mind at any time.

Storage used by this demo

Name Purpose Category Duration
agentic.sid (cookie) Session and CSRF request protection Strictly Necessary 8 hours; cleared at logout
agentic.consent (localStorage) Categories, timestamp, policy version Strictly Necessary 180 days; renewed when saved

Optional categories

Functional supports convenience features, Analytics measures usage, and Marketing supports advertising. This demo does not set cookies or load services in any optional category, even when enabled.

Withdraw or change consent

Use Cookie Settings in every footer to change or withdraw your choices. Rejecting optional storage does not prevent login. Clearing browser storage removes your record and shows the banner again.
