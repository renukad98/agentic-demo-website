POLICY VERSION 1.0

Privacy Notice

This is a local demonstration application. Do not enter sensitive or real personal data.

Information we hold

We store your name, email, bcrypt password hash, optional phone and address, account identifiers, timestamps, active status, Terms acceptance and Privacy Notice acknowledgment in a local JSON file. We use these details to provide login and profile editing. Sessions are held in server memory.

Purpose and legal basis

Account processing demonstrates performance of a service contract. Strictly necessary session storage supports authentication and request security. Optional cookie categories require your consent; this demo has no optional trackers or third-party integrations.

Retention and your rights

Demo user records remain until the operator deletes them. Sessions expire after eight hours or server restart. You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object where applicable. You may complain to your local supervisory authority. Withdraw optional cookie consent through Cookie Settings at any time.

Controller and contact

The local operator is responsible for this demo. A real controller identity, postal address, contact channel, retention schedule and applicable jurisdiction must be supplied before public use. Contact the person who provided this installation for data requests. No data is sold or sent to an external analytics service.
